                    Sources & method

                    Where every number comes from

                    Data sources

                    • Sectoral deployment of bank credit: RBI monthly release (SIBC return, banks covering ~95% of non-food credit).
                    • Deposits and credit totals: RBI fortnightly Scheduled Banks' Statement of Position, month-end editions.
                    • Lending and deposit rates, MCLR, benchmark mix: RBI monthly Lending and Deposit Rates of SCBs.
                    • Cards, ATMs, PoS, QR codes: RBI Bank-wise ATM/POS/Card Statistics.
                    • CPI inflation: MoSPI API (base 2012, to Dec 2025) and MoSPI/PIB monthly releases (base 2024, from Jan 2026).
                    • Repo rate: RBI Monetary Policy Committee decisions.
                    • Rural, semi-urban, urban and metro split; states: RBI quarterly Spatial Distribution of Deposits and Credit (DBIE, Statements 1 and 3A), SCBs excluding regional rural banks, from March 2023.
                    • CASA, AUM, stage 3 and coverage ratios: companies' quarterly investor presentations filed on NSE (a results press release where the presentation is only on the company website). Figures are read by Claude and kept only when the quoted text is found on the cited page and the value is in a plausible range.
                    • Share prices: NSE daily bhavcopy (closing prices) for market cap, price-to-book and P/E.
                    • Bank, NBFC and HFC results: standalone quarterly financial results filed with NSE in XBRL (old results format to Dec 2024, SEBI Integrated Filing from Mar 2025). NBFCs and HFCs file the Ind AS format.

                    Caveats

                      Refreshing the data

                      • python src/run_all.py runs everything: new RBI releases (through a headless browser where RBI's file server blocks scripts), CPI releases and repo rate, NSE results for banks and NBFCs, then the database and this page.
                      • A Windows scheduled task runs it every evening; each run's summary is in logs/last_run.json.
                      • Not yet included: loan mix and branch counts for banks, and product mix for NBFCs. CASA, AUM and stage 3 ratios come from investor presentations, so a lender shows them only once its latest deck has been read.
